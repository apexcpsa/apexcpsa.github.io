Our story

Engineering built from the ground up

Apex Consulting Projects (Pty) Ltd was founded in 2026 by Mr Netshandama Oda with a simple goal: to give South African industry a responsive, technically sharp partner that genuinely understands how plants, mines and municipal systems work on the ground.

Our foundation is practical, on-site experience — calibrating field instruments, maintaining PLC and DCS systems, wiring and commissioning control panels, and keeping critical electrical and water infrastructure running. That experience shapes how we work: we listen first, diagnose properly and fix things to last.

Today Apex operates across four integrated divisions — Electrical; Automation, Process Control & Instrumentation; Water & Wastewater Treatment; and Engineering & Projects — allowing clients to rely on one accountable team for multi-disciplinary work.
